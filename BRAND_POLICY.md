# GhostLink brand policy

This policy covers the GhostLink name, logo and product-identifying artwork. It does not change the [Apache-2.0 source license](LICENSE).

## Describing the technology

You may make truthful descriptive references to GhostLink and preserve required source attribution. For example, an accurate statement that a product “integrates the GhostLink SDK” identifies the technology without claiming to be the official GhostLink service.

Keep the distinction clear between your product, the source project and the official hosted service. Do not imply that a fork, deployment, token, bot or service is approved, audited, operated or endorsed by GhostLink unless that is actually authorized.

## Branding another product

This repository grants no general permission to adopt the GhostLink logo or product-identifying artwork as the branding of another product, to use confusing official-service claims, or to extract branded graphics from the documentation screenshots for marketing assets.

Use your own branding for a separately operated product. Permission for additional GhostLink asset use should be requested from the maintainers of [Ghostlink-Labs/ghostlink](https://github.com/Ghostlink-Labs/ghostlink). A public permission request should contain no credentials or private materials.

## Screenshots and legal scope

The four documentation screenshots have their own [image terms](docs/media/screenshots/LICENSE.md). Their publication does not publish the application source, design-system files or editable logos.

This policy does not assert that a trademark is registered, create rights where applicable law grants none, or restrict lawful descriptive references, criticism, commentary or other statutory rights. It does not prohibit reuse of Apache-licensed code, general interface ideas or independently created designs.
